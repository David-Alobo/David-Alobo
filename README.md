### Featured project: [DockPrepBench](https://github.com/David-Alobo/DockPrepBench)

**How much does preparation matter in molecular docking, compared with the choice of engine?**

A benchmark that changes one preparation choice at a time (search box, ligand protonation, start conformer) and redocks all 428 complexes of the PoseBusters set with AutoDock Vina, Smina, Gnina and rDock, comparing every arm with its baseline complex by complex.

- Preparation moves top-1 success by 7 to 10.5 points within an engine, so small differences between engines can be preparation noise.
- The search box matters most: a tight box raised Vina from 46.3% to 53.0%.
- Paired statistics with Holm correction and a seed noise floor; open code, configurations and per-complex results.

[Results explorer: every complex, engine and pose in 3D →](https://david-alobo.github.io/DockPrepBench/)
